About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at Auburn University. My research interests include the economics of crime, labor economics, and applied microeconomics. I am especially interested in how criminal justice policies affect access to alternatives to traditional prosecution and subsequent criminal justice outcomes.

Job Market Paper

Impact of Pre-Arrest Diversion Program in Criminal Justice System
Job Market Paper

This paper studies a pre-arrest diversion program that offers eligible individuals an alternative to traditional criminal case processing. The project examines how access to diversion affects criminal justice outcomes and subsequent contact with the criminal justice system.

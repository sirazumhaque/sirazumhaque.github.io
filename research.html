Impact of Pre-Arrest Diversion Program in Criminal Justice System
Job Market Paper

This paper studies a pre-arrest diversion program that offers eligible individuals an alternative to traditional criminal case processing. The project examines how access to diversion affects criminal justice outcomes and subsequent contact with the criminal justice system.

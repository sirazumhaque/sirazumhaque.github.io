Teaching

I have extensive experience teaching undergraduate economics as both an instructor of record and a teaching assistant. At Auburn University, I have served as instructor of record for Principles of Microeconomics, Principles of Macroeconomics, and Money and Banking across multiple terms and in both in-person and asynchronous online formats. My teaching emphasizes clear economic intuition, applied examples, structured practice, and an accessible learning environment in which students with different levels of preparation can engage confidently with economics.
